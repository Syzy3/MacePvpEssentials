# Changelog

All notable changes to Mace PvP Essentials are listed here. Version numbers match the versions published on [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/macepvpessentials/versions).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-11

Initial release for Minecraft 26.2 (Fabric Loader 0.19.3+), Fabric API.

### Visuals
- Breach and Density maces get their own slot color, with a right-click color picker and opacity.
- Colored background behind Totems of Undying, with the same picker.
- Shield status: shields turn green while they're ready and red while they're disabled, your own and other players'.
- Smash damage next to the crosshair, including while flying and before you swap to your mace. Choose when it shows and which side it sits on.
- Hitboxes: off, on every entity, or only on players flying with an elytra.
- Sizes for the first-person shield, the offhand totem and the totem pop animation.
- Minecraft's Particles and Damage Tilt options in the mod's menu, plus a **None** particle level.

### Crosshair
- Crosshair editor with a live preview: five styles, any color and opacity, length, thickness, gap, center dot, outline, a separate color while aiming at a player, a third-person crosshair, and cooldown and elytra indicators.

### Game Optimization
- Mace, Pearl, Wind Charge and Rocket optimization show your action the moment you take it. All off by default.
- Opponent Delay: how far behind their real position other players are drawn.
- Combat FPS: lighter video settings while it's on, without touching your own settings in `options.txt`.

### Other
- Recommended and Reset preset buttons, each behind a confirmation.
- Cancel puts every setting back to how it was when you opened the menu.
- A switch under the title turns the whole mod off.
- The Kinetic button on the pause screen, and settings from Mod Menu.
- An **Open Mace PvP Essentials** key, unbound by default, rebindable in the mod's settings or in Options → Controls.
